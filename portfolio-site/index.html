National Bank of Ethiopia · Akaki EIFS campus

A whole campus, crashed into twelve months.

The EIFS campus renovation and scale-up project (about USD 20 million): more than 40 blocks renovated or built at once, from dormitories to an auditorium, sports courts and guard towers. I run its baseline, look-aheads and the internal crashed programme that pulls completion to 28 Aug 2026.

What the crashed programme carries

Renders and the 112-sheet A1 drawing set (May 2026) are by the EEC design team; the programme, crash and look-aheads are my work.

  • Baseline. 691 activities, 1,270 logic links, 41 resources and 2,154 assignments across 226 WBS elements, 29 Aug 2025 to 28 Aug 2026.
  • Crash. An internal 680-activity crashed version with 1,136 links, re-sequenced so blocks run with parallel crews.
  • Progress at 24 Mar 2026. 64 activities complete and 92 under way, so the crash is tested against real site output.
  • Into 4D. The same crashed schedule, 899 tasks, was exported to SYNCHRO for model-based sequencing.

Arba Minch Stadium · EoT claim

Payment delay drives the claim: 36 days, not 68.

Time impact analysis in P6 for the stadium programme. I modelled each delay event as a fragnet in its own copy of the schedule, then ran them together to test concurrency.

  • Fragnet 1: undefined scope and late design information (259 days).
  • Fragnet 2: late payment of interim certificates (335 days).
  • Three scenario models of 799 activities each, 31 resources, 7-day site calendar.
  • Design delay alone pushes completion 32 days; payment alone 36. Run together they overlap, so the cumulative entitlement is 36 days, the figure a reviewer can trust.

Bishoftu International Airport · Earthworks Package 2

A force majeure window, measured against the 7 Jan 2027 finish.

For the earthworks of Africa's largest planned airport, I inserted the force majeure (pre-legislation) event into the progressed programme at the 1 Apr 2026 data date and measured its effect on completion.

  • 820 activities, 1,398 relationships, 26 resources and 3,344 assignments.
  • Eight calendars, including separate rainy-season and two-shift calendars for the earthmoving fleets.
  • 409 activities complete and 162 in progress at the data date: the analysis sits on real progress, not a plan.
  • The impacted forecast is 8 Feb 2027, 32 days past the required completion.

EAH apartment and parking complex · Recovery programme No. 2

Four weekly updates, seven days lost every week.

Trend analysis across four consecutive updates of a 2,048-activity recovery programme: nine G+20+R apartment towers and three G+6+T parking buildings.

  • 2,272 relationships, 132 resources and 22,389 resource assignments.
  • Completion slid from 19 Apr to 10 May 2027 in three weeks, while completed activities rose only from 254 to 292.
  • Reading the slope, not the single date, is what tells management the recovery plan is not recovering.

    How I use it

    • Model. Revit and AutoCAD models, brought in through the SYNCHRO plug-ins for Revit, Navisworks and MicroStation.
    • Link. Import the P6 programme as XML and map tasks to model elements, with temporary works and equipment from a 220-model library.
